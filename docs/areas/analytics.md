# Reports and analytics

The internal report desk lives at `/w/[workspaceSlug]/analytics`. It reads existing source records and writes only saved report presets and labelled one-off cash-flow assumptions. The single additive migration is `20261021020000_analytics`. Workspace deletion cascades to both new models. No external connector, chart dependency, accounting posting, parsing service or member-facing tool is added. Secrets are excluded.

## Reading and checking a report

Choose month to date, last month, quarter to date, the April–March Indian financial year to date, or a custom inclusive period of up to two years. Current periods stop on the workspace-local date. Comparisons use the preceding calendar month/quarter/financial year through the same elapsed day, clamped to that period’s end; complete periods compare complete periods. Custom ranges use the preceding equal number of days, with complete calendar years aligned to calendar years. Last-year dates clamp leap days. Currency selection never converts or adds unlike currencies. Money stays in integer minor units at rest; aggregate arithmetic uses PostgreSQL numeric/bigint and checks safe JavaScript totals.

The overview and six area tabs use one reviewed SQL definition registry. Each metric defines its source, date basis, numerator, denominator, group, permissions and supported dimensions. Fund, cost-centre, team, project and programme filters apply only where the source has a structured relationship; unsupported metrics disappear rather than silently becoming workspace totals. People and volunteer team filters use all memberships, rather than choosing an arbitrary first team. Programmes are the existing Funding records and Finance cost centres; no parallel programme register exists.

Comparison labels open the corresponding prior/last-year record period. Event totals retain baseline-only groups at zero current value, so a disappeared spending category remains eligible for the largest-change insight; rates and averages never invent observations. Short periods use daily sparkline points; longer periods use monthly points. Headline values and chart groups open the exact underlying fact list, with stable 50-row pagination and links to the source tool. Source screens without a detail route use exact-record filters or anchors. Every metric, underlying list and forecast has a private, no-store, formula-neutralised CSV export. Fact exports refuse more than 10,000 records; grouped query output is bounded at 10,000 rows and asks for a narrower period. Exports are audited. No record data is cached across viewers.

The SVG chart kit includes bars, stacked progress, lines, donuts, sparklines, funnels and calendar strips. It uses design tokens, linked keyboard-focusable marks, textual descriptions, titles and an expandable complete data table. Signed cash has a zero baseline. Large charts show at most forty groups while retaining all groups in the table and export. Forms use shared labelled controls, native keyboard disclosures and 44px actions, wrapping at narrow widths. The main empty state uses Patch. Browsers are prohibited by the task, so interactive 375px and screen-reader operation require human review; no browser check is claimed.

## Metrics

Finance covers posted income by source/funder; spending by category, vendor, fund and cost centre; net programme/project statements; full-period budget actuals and utilisation; restricted fund spending; petty-cash vouchers; recorded GST components and withholding; legacy and controlled receivable ageing; supplier payable ageing; fixed/estimated recurring monthly costs; purchase/claim commitments; controlled documents; and explicitly gated paid payroll and remittance balances. The ageing bands are 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days, with not-yet-overdue balances in the first band. Current outstanding balances are not a reconstruction of later payments at a historical date. Budget tables retain actual numerator, envelope denominator and remaining amount.

Funding covers received cash donations, average gift, campaigns against goals, top donors, adjacent-period retention cohorts, grant stages/value, expected instalments, report/deliverable deadlines, restricted fund utilisation, impact-entry evidence and latest saved BOM estimates against linked awards. Cash gifts exclude in-kind estimates; anonymous identities do not enter retention denominators. Donors are distinct by contact and currency. New, retained and lapsed cohorts use equal adjacent periods. Donation logging does not post a ledger entry, so donations are never added again to accounting income. Campaign/fund/BOM ratios retain their numerator and denominator and keep currencies separate. Awards and current register values are not promised cash.

People covers current headcount by department and engagement, joiners/leavers, recorded attendance, approved leave, approved volunteer hours, hiring stages, linked onboarding time-to-hire and certification expiry. Attendance excludes approved AWAY requests with surviving availability records, follows self/direct-manager/admin readership, and does not treat missing days as absence. A half-day contributes 0.5. Volunteer hours have person/team relationships; the source has no programme relationship, so programme hours are not inferred from free text.

Work covers current project health, due milestone completion, visible task creation/completion, overdue tasks, current blockers and task-age proxy, shipped releases, bug creation/current resolution by severity, visible meeting hours and meeting-action completion. Operations covers help volume, staff first response, resolution time and resolution-SLA misses; safety reports/follow-ups; retained hourly monitoring availability; incident MTTR; security incidents; completed access reviews; authoritative device assignments and licence seats; compliance completion; risk ratings; renewing contracts/subscriptions; knowledge verification, responsibilities and recovery tests. Safeguarding shows counts/status only for assigned cases with a current committee appointment and is excluded from report packs. Marketing covers dated content published/planned, reach/engagement samples, proposals and outcomes, closed-outcome win rate and open proposal value.

## Source limitations and consistency audit

Current-state measures are explicitly labelled and have no historical percentage comparison. Source rows can be reopened, archived or removed, and the permitted schema adds no history warehouse. Milestones lack actual completion timestamps, so their current completion ratio is not labelled an on-time hit rate. Blockers lack a start timestamp, so their task age is a labelled proxy. Bug resolution uses last update as a labelled proxy. Help desk stores a resolution target, not a first-response target; unanswered requests are excluded from first-response averages. Governance obligations lack completion timestamps, so exact on-time/late filing cannot be established and completion status is reported. Training means the existing dated certification evidence, not invented attendance. Monitoring uses retained hourly rollups only, includes up and degraded samples in availability to match Services, and does not assume missing samples are healthy.

Finance report ledger predicates match analytics: tenant, currency, non-void and non-private-payroll entries. A database consistency test compares actual report totals against analytics. Budget actuals share `lib/finance/reportMetrics.ts`: category/cost-centre selection takes precedence over explicit budget tags, matching `budgetActualWhere`; the report and budget list now batch their actuals instead of issuing one query per budget. Controlled receivables follow Documents allocation, retained credit and refund rules; legacy invoices stay separate to avoid adding linked source and controlled invoice balances. Funding impact SUM/LATEST and existing project completion logic were audited against their source contracts; analytics labels the available date basis rather than altering source facts. Money-control cash-only movements affect account cash, not expense totals.

## Cash-flow assumptions

The next twelve weeks and six months start the day after the workspace-local as-of date. Opening cash is the sum of account opening balances plus all non-void account ledger entries and cash-only movements through that date, respecting each account opening date. Unassigned ledger entries do not affect account cash. If no account opening balance is recorded for the currency, the screen states that zero is assumed, suppresses runway/threshold judgements and the pack cash chart, and keeps the missing balance visible. Opening cash remains aggregate; forecasts require finance administrators and clear area filters because accounts do not have allocated opening fund/project balances.

Unpaid bills, approved disbursements, projected active recurring schedules, eligible planned payroll, expected grant instalments, active pledges and manual adjustments supply dated lines. Existing recurring dues prevent projecting an issued bill again. Schedule projection clamps due days, respects end dates/EMI tenure and includes configured tax/withholding cash amounts. Overdue commitments move into the first future bucket. Unknown payroll dates use a stated day-28 assumption. Payroll obligations require the viewer's explicit grant; omission is stated when absent. Budgets are displayed as overlapping envelopes and excluded from dated cash to avoid counting both a budget and its bills. Undated uncommitted spending can be entered as a manual adjustment. Pledges and grant instalments must not describe the same receipt twice.

Forms accept ordinary currency amounts, with currency-specific decimal precision, and convert to signed integer minor units before saving. Positive adjustments add cash; negative adjustments spend cash. They never create or edit a ledger entry. Up to twenty private proof files per adjustment use `uploadWorkspaceFile`; selection, drag/drop and phone-camera capture accept images/PDFs, with ten files per batch, 10 MB per file and 24 MB combined, subject to the shared configured policy and image decode validation. Images/PDFs preview through the authorised file route. Orphans and revoked viewers fail closed. `/analytics` files are reserved outside library reads, mutations and retention.

`lib/scheduled/analytics.ts` registers with the existing worker/beat. It sends a generic personal-only due-date review prompt through `dispatch()`, only to current active admins. Tenant locks and a durable event key deduplicate concurrent ticks. It never automatically confirms or posts an assumption.

## Saved reports and packs

Save a section, filters and period with a name, optional pin and commentary. Private views belong to their author; admins may share the view with other admins. Every reader re-runs their own source permissions. Search indexes preset names PRIVATE/owner-scoped and assumption titles PRIVATE/admin-scoped, with live source gates. Proof contents, commentary, donor tax identities, salaries and case contents never enter search or activity.

An administrator can prepare a management or board/funder print page and an immutable generated PDF snapshot using the existing Gilroy PDF helper. Both use the same metric reader, include period comparisons, commentary, charts and source-method notes, and exclude private payroll, donor rankings, confidential requests, private meetings and safeguarding. Saved PDFs use existing private object storage and stay private to the generating preset owner even when the view is shared. Downloads recheck current active admin membership, tenant and record linkage. A preset holds up to twenty generated snapshots. Admins may enable monthly management PDF email on a shared Last month preset and select current admin recipients. Beat queues one private stored PDF through the existing outbox per preset/month. Recipient and owner membership, current sharing/recipient choice, file linkage and checksum are rechecked at delivery; revoked access or a disabled schedule prevents delivery. Generic due reminders continue through dispatch. Live SMTP delivery is not exercised by tests.
