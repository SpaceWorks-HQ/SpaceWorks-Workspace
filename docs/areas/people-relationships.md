# People and relationships

This area replaces the members, teams, rota, contacts and onboarding placeholders.

Members can search the directory by name, skill, team or workspace role; profiles show workspace-specific bio, pronouns, time zone, skills, interests, visible teams, recent shared activity, training and shoutout totals. Message and shoutout links use the receiving areas' `?to=` contracts. Profile edits are tenant-specific, stored in `MemberProfile.handles.profile` so changing one workspace does not alter another workspace's profile. Admin title and notes use `handles.title` and `handles.adminNotes`; notes never enter search or activity.

Emergency contacts are write-only during editing, encrypted with `member-emergency`, and revealed only on explicit request by the member or an admin. Every successful view, including an empty contact, writes an audit event in the same transaction. Reveals never enter page payloads, search, activity or notifications. Clearing an emergency contact is supported. Suspended members cannot reveal contacts.

Admins create, edit, archive and restore teams. Team URLs remain stable when names change. Leads manage their own team's members and roles. Members join public teams or leave their current teams. Private teams are hidden from outsiders, including directory filters. Team pages list visible channels and projects; channel membership still applies.

Time-off requests use CUSTOM checklist runs containing an `availability_request` item. Pending requests are visible to the requester and admins. Approval creates an Availability row; rejection cancels the request. There is no schema change. Advisory locks prevent overlapping approvals and row locks prevent double approval. Approved availability respects WORKSPACE, TEAM and PRIVATE visibility, and appears in a seven-day availability strip. Workspace time-zone day boundaries handle daylight saving changes.

Rota templates support teams, capacity, keyholder and training requirements. Admins and team leads schedule weekly shifts for up to twelve weeks. Templates can be edited and their default capacity is used when a schedule leaves capacity blank. Sign-up, dropping and cover swaps are transactional; per-user advisory locks plus shift row locks prevent overlapping sign-ups, capacity races and duplicate swap acceptance. Current sign-offs must remain valid through the shift's end. Admins define training types and trainer teams; authorised trainers grant sign-offs and admins revoke them.

Onboarding templates support subject, user, role and team-lead assignees, deadlines and required items. Invitation acceptance starts matching templates and emits `member.joined` in the membership transaction. Team joins start matching team templates. Admin membership-type changes start matching membership-type templates. Manual assignment is also available. Offboarding reads vault ownership/grants/access logs, service ownership, equipment checkouts, keys, subscriptions, external accounts, teams and tasks; it generates referenced handover items without changing those records or reading vault values. Offboarding runs are admin-only. Completion can suspend membership, with the shared owner-row lock and role checks preserving an active owner.

CRM routes are `/contacts`, `/contacts/[contactId]`, `/contacts/organisations`, `/contacts/organisations/[organisationId]` and `/contacts/pipeline`. The task's `/organisations` and `/pipeline` URLs redirect to those canonical routes. Records support visibility, owner, tags, notes, interactions and tenant-checked links. Members create and edit their own records; admins manage all records, remove records, import/export contact and organisation CSV, and export contact vCards. Sensitive phones are redacted for other members. Duplicate detection normalises email, phone and name for contacts, and website domain/name for organisations. The pipeline has six stages, accessible forms for stage changes, and integer minor-unit storage with currency-aware input and display.

CSV imports validate the entire bounded file before writing, reject malformed quoting and skip duplicates within the workspace and within the file. Exports escape quotes and neutralise spreadsheet formulas. vCards escape delimiters/newlines and fold UTF-8 lines to 75 octets. CRM search documents contain names/tags only and are indexed PRIVATE for owners because the shared search reader does not yet provide an area role gate. This intentionally limits global CRM search discovery; CRM's own search applies full access rules.

`lib/scheduled/people.ts` dispatches shift reminders, coverage gaps, checklist deadlines, certification expiry and contact follow-ups. Persistent event keys and transaction advisory locks deduplicate replica ticks. Notification text never contains emergency details, vault values or private checklist contents.

Demo data lives in `scripts/seed/people-relationships.mjs` and is loaded by the existing demo runner. Tests live in `tests/unit/people-relationships.test.ts` and `tests/integration/people-relationships.db.test.ts`; browser coverage is in `tests/e2e/people-relationships.spec.ts`.

Cross-area changes: the invitation acceptance hook in `lib/invitations.ts` is explicitly assigned to this area. Completing offboarding updates Membership status as required by the area task. Direct `/organisations` and `/pipeline` aliases are outside the roadmap's nested route list but explicitly requested by the task. No schema, shared navigation or deployment changes are required.
