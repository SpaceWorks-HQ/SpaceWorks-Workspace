# Organisation and people

Four manual internal tools extend the existing people, teams, profiles, leave and rota tools. There are no external connectors, payroll calculations or automated escalation calls.

- `/organisation`: departments with head, parent and notes; an indented department tree and reporting tree; organisation CSV export; member department/manager assignments; a direct “Reports to me” list. Indentation is capped on narrow screens and each row names its parent. Admins maintain structure. An admin cannot change an admin/owner's reporting line. Workspace owners can.
- `/responsibilities`: area/process, status, multiple responsible/consulted/informed members, one accountable member, backup, review date, reminder lead time and notes. Filter by title, status or “me”; CSV export; archive/restore through status. Profiles show all assigned RACI and backup roles. Guests cannot open these internal tools.
- `/attendance`: monthly per-person/per-team register for staff, interns and volunteers. Manual present, absent, half-day, remote and on-leave entry by the person, direct manager or authorised admin. Read access is self/direct manager/admin; editing preserves the existing admin hierarchy. Team filtering never expands this audience and private teams remain hidden. Missing days remain “Not recorded”. Present/remote count as 1 and half-day as 0.5; nothing calculates money. The CSV lists every permitted person/day, engagement type, status, attendance-day count and source. It omits notes and private contact details. Existing leave balances, shift assignments and volunteer-hour logs remain authoritative in their tools and are linked from Attendance.
- `/escalation`: “Who do I call?” lists ordered contacts for each area, in/out-of-hours availability, working hours/time zone, instructions and notes. Admins can reorder contacts with keyboard-accessible move buttons or pause a path. The reader filters by area and time and can export CSV; the export applies the same contact privacy rules. Links from Help desk and Safety lead here. No call, message or page is sent automatically.

Approved AWAY leave is overlaid dynamically from completed `availability_request` checklists and their surviving Availability row. Pending, declined, cancelled, busy, travel and standalone away rows do not become leave attendance. Dates are workspace-local calendar days. Leave notes never enter the attendance response; cancelling leave removes its pre-fill. Approved leave takes precedence over a prior manual attendance entry; the original entry is retained and returns if leave is cancelled. Correct/cancel the leave request before changing attendance on an approved leave day. Future attendance is rejected. A version timestamp prevents concurrent edits from silently overwriting each other.

Profiles now offer a phone, messaging handle and explicit contact visibility. The default is private to the person and admins, matching the existing profile privacy model. Opting into workspace sharing exposes these contacts only to members and above, including on escalation paths and profiles. Guests never receive another person's contacts. Emergency contacts are unchanged and are never used as escalation contacts. Removed/suspended/disabled people are displayed as former/unavailable without contact data.

All writes resolve the workspace from the authenticated slug, rate-limit, validate with Zod, recheck live membership inside a transaction, audit ids without private content and revalidate the area. Tenant-leading indexes and workspace cascades ship in `20261021011000_org_people`. Advisory locks prevent concurrent hierarchy cycles and duplicate daily records. Profile department and parent department foreign keys include the workspace. Other actor references follow the existing plain-id convention and are validated as active tenant members.

Attendance and responsibility records support up to 30 private image/PDF attachments, ten per batch and 25 MB combined, respecting the shared configured per-file limit and image decoder checks. Files go only through `uploadWorkspaceFile` into existing private object storage. File selection, drag/drop, camera capture and image/PDF previews are available. `/api/files/[id]` rechecks current record access, including a changed manager. Attendance writers and responsibility admins upload. Reserved `/org-people` orphans fail closed. Files stay with their record and are excluded from general library lists, trash, exports, moves and versions. This area generates no PDFs.

Search indexes department, responsibility and escalation titles with live member/status gates. Attendance indexes only a generic date title, PRIVATE to its subject; global attendance search is restricted to that person even for admins. No notes, leave details, contact data or file bytes enter search. Shared activity uses generic structural/register changes without titles, so guest-visible profile activity cannot disclose internal titles. `lib/scheduled/orgPeople.ts`, called from the existing registered people job, dispatches `people.responsibility_review_due` to current responsible/accountable/backup members. A persistent marker per record/review date and transaction lock make replicas/retries safe. Paused/archived responsibilities do not remind. Moving a review date creates a new reminder period. The notification carries ids and a generic title only.

The demo module `scripts/seed/zz-org-people.mjs` sorts after the existing people seed so it extends existing profiles. It adds a Kochi community office, programmes department, staff/intern/volunteer reporting lines, bank reconciliation/website/fire-safety responsibilities, mixed attendance and office-safety escalation. It is safe to invoke twice.
